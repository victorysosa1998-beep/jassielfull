How to Apply

Admission Process

01

visit the school and fill out theadmission form with all required information about your child.

02

Provide previous school reports, passport photos & guardian Details

Entrance Assessment

Sit for a brief entrance test in English and Mathematics at our campus.

04

What You'll Need

Admission Requirements

  • Completed Application Form
  • Last School Report Cards
  • Recent Passport Photographs
  • Primary School Leaving Certificate (for JSS 1 entry)
  • BECE / JSS3 Result (for SSS 1 entry)
  • Parent/Guardian Names
